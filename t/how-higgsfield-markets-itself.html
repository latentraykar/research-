How Higgsfield markets itself, and what Gulab can copy

Prepared for Kaustubh, October 5, 2026.
Sources are listed at the bottom.
All numbers are public claims or third-party estimates, not audited figures.

1. The short version

Higgsfield did not grow through one big launch.
It paid creators to make a small set of signature video formats.
Those formats then spread on their own, because people copied them.
Every new feature became its own launch, its own landing page and its own wave of creator posts.
The founder posts real numbers in public, which keeps the story moving between launches.

2. The five plays

Play 1: Pay creators for one specific, tagged move

  • Their "Earn" program paid creators to make videos with the product and tag the brand.
  • The company says that in the first 20 days, 10,000 creators submitted 50,000 videos.
  • Paid creators carry a visible #higgsfieldpartner tag on TikTok.
  • The lesson is that they paid for a specific format, not for general "awareness".

Play 2: Invent a signature format people want to copy

  • Their team created a few reusable moves: the satellite camera swoop, the eye zoom and the outfit morph.
  • These moves filled Instagram and TikTok feeds.
  • By the founder's account, celebrities and football clubs later posted content made with these moves on their own accounts.
  • The format became the ad, and other people paid to distribute it.

Play 3: Every launch goes out as a coordinated creator drop

  • Between March and June 2026, six product releases went out through a creator network.
  • The agency behind it reports about 40M views across those six drops.
  • Creators were chosen by their median post performance and audience fit, not by follower count.
  • Each creator wrote their own hook, so the posts did not look like one copied ad.
  • All posts went live inside the same four-hour window, so the platforms pushed them together.

Play 4: Demo-first content, never a feature list

  • Creator briefs asked for before-and-after comparisons, creative showcases and real workflows.
  • The rule was to show the tool inside real creative work, not a scripted walkthrough.
  • Their own Instagram uses "Comment X to get the link" to turn viewers into signups.

Play 5: The founder posts numbers, not updates

  • The CEO, Alex Mashrabov, runs the build-in-public story on his personal X and LinkedIn.
  • He posts concrete numbers, like revenue run rate and usage, instead of general news.
  • Their YouTube channel posts tutorials and workflows, which act as education and demand generation at the same time.

3. One warning

In February 2026, Higgsfield's X account was suspended for inauthentic behaviour.
There was also press criticism about creators in the Earn program not being paid.
So we copy the format and creator approach, but we keep it clean.
Every paid post is disclosed, and every creator is paid on time.

4. What this means for Gulab

Where talking-head reels fit

Higgsfield's feed is almost all product output.
A real human face explaining how a film was made is the gap.
Your talking-head reels can be the human layer on top of the AI output.

Suggested format mix for Gulab

1. Signature move reels. Pick two or three Gulab moves that look impossible and are easy to copy, for example a full film from one sentence, or one character kept consistent across ten shots. Repeat them until people know them.
2. Talking-head breakdowns (you on camera). "I made this ad in one hour, here is how." Hook in the first two seconds, Gulab output cut in, you explaining one decision.
3. Before and after. Reference reel on one side, Gulab version on the other. The cricket ad is a ready example.
4. Comment-to-get-the-link. End reels with "comment GULAB and I will send you the file".
5. Launch drops. Every new Gulab feature goes out as one coordinated drop: your reel, the brand post and a few creators, all in the same window.

What can start before the phone arrives

  • Choose the two or three signature moves.
  • Write the three talking-head scripts around them.
  • Make the AI output clips that the talking-head reels will cut to.
  • Build a short list of creators, chosen by median views, not followers.
