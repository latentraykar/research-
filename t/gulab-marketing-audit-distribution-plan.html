Gulab marketing audit + distribution plan

Where Gulab stands on every channel with real numbers, best selling points, weak points, the gap to Higgsfield and Kaye, and a steal-like-an-artist plan: pillars, hooks, comeback loops, a two-week calendar and KPIs.

1. Where we stand, on one screen

Gulab has the better product and almost no distribution. One canvas carries about 100 models across image, video, voice, music, sfx and lipsync, from $10 (₹799) a month. Higgsfield charges more, has less sound, and still wins because it posts about 16 reels a week to 248k people on X alone. We post a few times a month to 1.8k.

The numbers (read Oct 15 2026):

  • X @trygulab: 1,841 followers. 3 posts so far in October, 4 in September. The last 5 posts drew 174 to 994 views each.
  • YouTube @trygulab: 97 subscribers, 8 videos, last upload 3 months ago.
  • Instagram @trygulab: logged-out reads are blocked, so no follower count from here. Need 5 numbers from the app (section 3).
  • Signups, last 30 days: 371. 289 of them (78%) came with no source tag, so we can't tell which channel brought them.
  • Tagged signups, last 30 days: paid X campaign 49, auto-DM link 18, Instagram 12, ChatGPT 3.
  • Only 49 of the 371 (13%) ran a single generation.

Three things fix most of this:
1. A recurring face plus one feature per reel, on IG and X every weekday. Higgsfield's job-skit format and Kaye's face, applied to our stack.
2. A keyword CTA on every reel, wired to the auto-DM. It already works: 18 signups since Oct 4 from the auto-DM link, more than Instagram's own link.
3. Tag every link we post. Without it we can't see which posts bring signups, and 78% of signups stay untraceable.

2. What we sell best, and where we're weak

Best selling points (all true on prod today):

  • A whole film with sound in one place. Image, video, voice, voice clone, dubbing, music, synced sfx (Mirelo) and lipsync on one canvas, with a timeline. Nobody else ships the sound side this complete. This is the line to lead with.
  • Cheapest paid entry. $10 / ₹799 a month vs Higgsfield's $15. UPI in India. Credits roll over for 60 days.
  • Every top model, one balance. Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0, Hailuo 03, Nano Banana, GPT Image 2 and more. New models land on prod within an hour of release.
  • An agent that runs the canvas. One prompt in, a whole canvas out (the v3 launch line).
  • Works where pros already are. Premiere, After Effects, Figma, Blender, plus MCP and the ChatGPT and Claude connectors.
  • Made in India, priced for India, while still competing on the global model list.

Weak points (honest):

  • No packaging. Users still write every camera move and look. Higgsfield sells presets with names (Cinema Studio, Marketing Studio, Soul ID). Ours is a blank canvas, which is powerful and scary.
  • No character lock. "Same face across 10 shots" is the first thing filmmakers ask for. Higgsfield has Soul ID. We have references.
  • Activation. 13% of new signups generate anything. Most signups never see what the product does.
  • No recurring face, no series. Every post is a one-off. Nothing gives people a reason to come back tomorrow.
  • Cadence collapsed after the launch run. X went from 25 posts in July to 4 in September and 3 so far in October. YouTube has been quiet for 3 months.
  • Posting depends on handoffs. Videos get made, then wait for a poster. Posts 1 and 3 from Oct 12 never went live. Every reel that misses its slot is a reel that never existed.
  • We can't see what works. No source tags on most links, no shared numbers sheet, Instagram stats locked behind the login.

5. Steal like an artist: the distribution plan

Pillars (every post fits one)

1. Order the AI (Higgsfield's best format). Someone barks a job at Gulab in line one ("I need a trailer by 6pm"), and a visible asset lands every 5 to 8 seconds. 60 to 75s. This format gets 14 comments per 1k views on Higgsfield, twice their average.
2. One model, one feature, one reel. Every model launch gets a same-day walk-through: "Seedance 2.5 just landed on Gulab, look at this", then 5 to 7 escalating demos. We already ship models within the hour, so post at the same speed.
3. Sound is our flag. Mute-to-unmute reels: silent clip, then voice, music and sfx land one by one. Nobody else can show this in one place.
4. Tentpole shorts. Tales of Shiva, the Acheri short. Cinematic, cold open, Gulab only on the end card. This is where 1M-view reels come from on both accounts.
5. You, the face (Kaye's engine). Same person, same set, same sign-off. Talking-head scripts are already written (three in the hub).
6. The prompt drop (Kaye's comment engine). One-prompt tutorials: "comment PROMPT and I'll send it". This feeds the auto-DM.

Formats per channel

  • IG Reels: pillars 1 to 6, vertical, 25 to 75s. Caption line one is a headline with a famous name in it ("Gulab x Seedance 2.5 for trailers"), the keyword underneath. Never open the caption with "Comment".
  • X: the same reel cut to 30 to 45s, native upload. No link in the main post, link in the first reply. Model launches go out as a thread: reel, 3 outputs, then the link.
  • LinkedIn: carousels (Top 5 models, cost of a 30s film, before and after) and the founder story. Your profile beats the company page.
  • YouTube: every reel goes up as a Short the same day, plus tentpole films as long-form. Zero extra production.

Hook bank (first line, first 2 seconds)

  • "I need a 30-second trailer by 6pm. Go."
  • "This entire ad cost less than a coffee."
  • "Same prompt, five models. One of them is cheating."
  • "Turn the sound on." (silent open, pillar 3)
  • "Seedance 2.5 dropped an hour ago. It's already on Gulab."
  • "I made a film with no camera, no crew and no editor."
  • "Your brand, 10 ads, one canvas, 4 minutes."
  • "Stop paying for 6 AI tools."
  • "Comment STORYBOARD and I'll send you this exact setup."
  • "Day 3 of making a short film with only AI." (series)

Comeback loops (why people return)

  • Daily series with a number: "Day 7 of building a film on Gulab". People follow to see the next day.
  • Model Monday: every Monday, the best new model of the week, tested on one prompt.
  • Sound-on Friday: pillar 3, every Friday.
  • Remake requests: "comment a movie scene and I'll remake it on Gulab". The best comment becomes the next reel and tags the commenter.
  • Monthly offer with a deadline: one model free or unlimited for 7 days (Rohit or Shantnu decide what we can give). This is the highest comment-rate format on Higgsfield, at 15 per 1k views.
  • A home for users' work: a Discord or a weekly reshare of user films. Higgsfield's Discord is 213k people who come back every day.

Cadence

  • IG: 2 reels every weekday (10 a week). X: same reels plus launch threads. YouTube Shorts: mirrors IG. LinkedIn: 2 a week.
  • Timing: product reels 9am to 3pm ET (6:30pm to 12:30am IST), creative reels late US night, from the 200-reel teardown.
  • Test: post two cuts of the same reel the same day, keep the winner, rework the loser's hook.

Fixes before day one

1. Tag every link: `?utm_source=ig|x|li|yt&utm_campaign=<post-name>`. The auto-DM already does this, extend it everywhere. Then we can read signups per post in the database.
2. One poster with the logins, posting on time. Posts that wait on a handoff don't go out.
3. A shared numbers sheet: per post views, comments, link taps, signups. Read weekly.
4. Fix the landing for social traffic: someone tapping from a reel should land on the canvas the reel showed, not the empty canvas. Starter canvases exist; link reels to them.
