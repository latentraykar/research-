Gulab vs Higgsfield: model catalog

Model by model: what their catalog has that we don't (Soul, DOP, Seedance 2.5, newer Kling/Wan), what only we have (Veo, Sora, image edit, voice, music, canvas, API), and the gaps a filmmaker feels first.

catalog comparison: open-higgsfield vs gulab.

sources
open-higgsfield: its catalog folder at the latest commit (sep 17).
gulab: our model registry in the repo as of sep 14. prod may have moved a little since.

size
open-higgsfield: 38 entries. 8 image, 30 video. nothing else.
gulab: 113 entries across 13 categories. 30 text-to-image, 11 image edit, 24 image-to-video, 19 text-to-video, 2 video-to-video, 5 voices, 4 music, 1 sfx, 1 lipsync, 10 llms, 3 3d, 3 utility (upscale, video upscale, background remove).
some gulab video models appear in both the image-to-video and text-to-video lists, so unique models are fewer than 113.

what they have that we do not
soul 2 and soul cinema. these are higgsfield's own image models and only exist on their platform.
dop, their camera-move model. same story.
seedance 2.5, plus seedance 2.5 edit and extend. we stop at seedance 2.0.
kling 3.0 motion control and kling 3.0 4k. we have kling v3 pro and turbo, not those two.
wan 2.6, 2.7, 3.0 and 3.0 prime. we have wan v2 only.
ltx 2.5. we have ltx 2.3.
minimax h3 and hailuo 2.3. we have hailuo 02.
flux 3 video, grok imagine 2.0 image, z-image turbo, kling o1.

what we have that they do not
veo 3 and veo 3.1. sora 2 and sora 2 pro. gemini omni flash. none of these are in their catalog.
the whole nano banana family, gpt image 2.0 and 1.5, imagen 4, seedream 5, reve, krea, sensenova.
image editing as a category. they have zero edit models. we have 11.
video-to-video, voices, music, sfx, lipsync, llms, 3d, upscaling. they have none of these.

structure
they have one prompt bar and a browser-only history of 60 runs.
we have a node canvas, projects on the server, an api, an mcp server and plugins.
they store your key in a cookie and every run bills higgsfield credits.
we bill our own credits.

honest read
their video list is wider on kling, wan, seedance and minimax versions.
our list is far wider everywhere else, and it is the only one of the two that can build a full film with sound.
the only truly exclusive thing on their side is soul and dop. everything else is a vendor model we could also add.

what i would act on
check whether seedance 2.5 edit and extend, and kling 3 motion control, are available to us on fal. i have not checked that yet.
those are the gaps a filmmaker would feel first.
