Upsurge Content Framework

Your framework, written up. Sep 20, 2026.
Status: prep. Nothing goes live until Sowmay clears it.

The principle

Content is about what the product is for, not about the product.
The audience comes for the subject; the product is mentioned second, as the thing that made it possible.
We show the work: what we made, how we made it, and how you can make it too.
Every piece ends with something the viewer can act on.

The four content shapes

1. Made this. A finished piece, shown first, no setup. The product is named in the caption or the last line.
2. Here is how. The same piece, taken apart: the steps, the tools, the time it took, the mistakes.
3. You can too. A template, prompt, routine or checklist the viewer can copy today.
4. The making-of series (Banana Productions). One idea, a few days of work, the result, the learnings, the project link, the edit time. Long form first, cut down for Reels.

Theme per project

Gulab: AI filmmaking. We made this film, here is how, you can make yours.
Naksha: astrology. Compatibility, timings, "we found each other on Naksha". The app comes second.
Jawd: lookmaxxing and face fitness. Jawline routines, before and after, what actually works and what is a myth. The product is the tool inside the routine.
Banana Productions: the making-of label for every film and build across the lab.
Upsurge: building a lab that runs many bets at once. This is the exception: the company page carries company facts, not the about-not-app rule.
Lauki: an AI you can actually talk to as a member of the room. Only the three public claims from lauki.chat/privacy, nothing else.
OpenBio: biology in plain English. Research stories, product second. Only what openbio.tech already says publicly.

Not in the framework yet: Zelta until it is cleared, and anything not announced.

What every post must carry

Hook: the first line states the result or the claim; no setup.
Proof: something real we can show or point to. A number only if it is cleared for public use.
Product mention: second, never first, except on the Upsurge page.
CTA: one of three, follow, try it, or talk to us. Pick one per post.
Asset: the post names the visual it needs before it is drafted.

Pipeline

1. LinkedIn, Upsurge page. Company facts, launches, the lab story.
2. Reels. Instagram first, then the same cuts elsewhere. The about-not-app rule applies here.
3. YouTube long form. Gulab and Banana Productions. Shorts pull from the long cuts.

Rules that do not move

Every post comes to Lauki before it goes up, in every channel, indefinitely.
Public material only: our sites, our public posts, announcements. Nothing from internal chats.
No infrastructure, tools or vendor names.
No client or user names without consent on record.
Nothing goes live until Sowmay clears it.

Weekly loop

Monday: the week's list, one line per post, theme and shape marked.
Drafts land in this DM with the asset line.
Lauki returns sign-off plus the visual.
Post by hand. Numbers reported the following Monday.
