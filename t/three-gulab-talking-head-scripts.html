Three Gulab talking-head scripts

Written for Kaustubh, October 5, 2026.
Built on the Higgsfield teardown: hook in the first two seconds, one signature move per reel, a real face explaining one decision, and a comment-to-get-the-link ending.
Each reel runs about 30 to 45 seconds, in vertical 9:16.
"TH" means you on camera. "CUT" means Gulab output cut in.

Script 1: "I made this ad in one hour"

Signature move: a full ad from one reference reel.
Example to use: the Jawd cricket ad.

  • 0-2s TH: "This cricket ad took me one hour. No crew, no shoot."
  • 2-6s CUT: The finished ad's best three seconds, full screen.
  • 6-14s TH: "I started with a reel I liked. I gave Gulab the reference and told it to match the cuts and the beat."
  • 14-22s CUT: Split screen, the reference reel on the left and the Gulab version on the right.
  • 22-32s TH: "The one thing I changed myself was the casting. I locked the batsman and the commentators first, so every shot stayed consistent."
  • 32-40s TH: "Comment GULAB and I will send you the project file."

Script 2: "Same character, ten shots"

Signature move: one character that stays the same across every shot.

  • 0-2s TH: "Every AI video tool breaks on this. Watch."
  • 2-8s CUT: Fast montage of ten shots with the same character, different angles and locations.
  • 8-16s TH: "The hard part of AI film is not one good shot. It is keeping the same face for a whole story."
  • 16-24s CUT: The character sheet, then the shots built from it.
  • 24-34s TH: "In Gulab I build the cast first. Then every scene pulls from that cast, so the face does not drift."
  • 34-42s TH: "Comment CAST and I will show you how to set one up."

Script 3: "From one sentence to a film"

Signature move: a short film from a single line of text.

  • 0-2s TH: "I typed one sentence. This is what came back."
  • 2-10s CUT: The finished short film, best moments.
  • 10-18s TH, with the sentence on screen: "That was the whole prompt. Gulab wrote the script, then the shots, then the cut."
  • 18-28s CUT: Fast run through the stages: script, storyboard, shots, final cut.
  • 28-36s TH: "My job was to approve each stage. That is where the taste comes in."
  • 36-42s TH: "Comment FILM with your sentence and I will make the best one."

Notes for the shoot

  • Shoot all three in one session, same outfit and same setup, so they look like a series.
  • Use natural window light facing you, with the phone at eye level.
  • Record the hook three ways and keep the strongest one.
  • All three can be cut before the phone arrives, except the talking-head parts.
  • The "comment" endings only work if someone answers the comments, so decide who replies before posting.
