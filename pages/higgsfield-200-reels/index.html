The playbook in one paragraph

Higgsfield runs its Instagram as a launch machine with a film studio bolted on. Nearly every reel announces one thing (a model, a plugin, a feature or an offer). The first caption line pins it to a famous name ("Higgsfield x Claude Opus 5.5", "GPT-6 Astra + Higgsfield = AGI in gaming"), and the video proves it in a skit where a stressed person orders an AI to do a real job and it delivers on screen. Every reel ends on a keyword picked for that product ("comment SEEDANCE for the link"). 106 different keywords appear across the 200 reels. For reach they drop a long cinematic short every few weeks (their biggest reel is a 115s gangster film at 3.6M). For comments they run free or unlimited offers with a deadline. They post about two a day on weekdays, timed to the US working day.

Hook formulas (opening line, from the audio)

  1. The order to the AI. A person barks a job at the AI in the first 3 seconds: "I need a motion promo video ASAP", "Make me a viral app. Make no mistakes", "Create a 200 page manga with these characters", "I want to launch paid ads". Works because the payoff is promised in line one. This family has their best median (411k) and 14 comments per 1k, 2x the account.
  2. "X is finally here. Look at this." A new model is named, then the first demo plays within 2 seconds. Nano Banana Pro for video, 1.45M.
  3. Impossible act, first person. "I can freeze time and web up whatever I want. Except this object doesn't exist, and neither do the people around me." The reveal that the footage is fake comes in the 5th second. Seedance unlimited, 1.46M.
  4. Secret trend. "Something weird is happening on Instagram and nobody's talking about it", "most people still don't realize how fast motion design is changing". AI influencers 761k, Claude motion design 1.06M.
  5. Cold open mid-fight. The film starts inside the conflict, often with swearing or an insult: "You are so unbelievably stupid", "Sir, please sign this, your meeting is at six". The product never appears in the hook. Cully Hill Boys 3.6M, the AI-influencer drama 1.16M, Relight 659k.
  6. Money shout. "One million dollars!" said three times, "MCP stands for Make Crazy Profits", "It cannot be free." Captions that mention money have the highest median of any caption type (537k, 9 reels).
  7. Postcard opener. "I'm standing in front of the most iconic mountain in the world." A travel-vlog voice over generated footage, and the twist is that none of it was filmed. Premiere and After Effects plugin, 1.03M.

Caption first line (the second hook)

  • A headline first, the keyword below it. Reels whose caption opens straight with "Comment X..." have a median of 250k. Reels with a headline first: 377k (124 vs 76 reels). This is the single clearest caption rule in the set.
  • "Introducing / Meet [thing]": 403k median (25 reels). "[Frontier model] + Higgsfield" co-brand: 402k (16). Naming any frontier model (Claude, GPT, Seedance, Nano Banana...): 396k (58).
  • Bold claims: "For the first time in history...", "...= AGI in gaming", "We gave Claude Opus 5.5 access to 12 laptops", "What if we taught a fruit fly to do motion design?"

The formula: replicate both

  1. One face, one product, every reel. Kaye proves a recurring human lead lowers the swipe rate. Higgsfield proves every reel should ship exactly one product thing. For Gulab, that means you (Kaustubh) as the recurring lead, with one Gulab feature or model per reel.
  2. Line 1 = the order or the reveal. Open on a spoken command to the AI ("I need a launch film by 6pm") or on the impossible act. The payoff is promised before second 3.
  3. Caption line 1 = headline with a famous name. "Meet Gulab x [model] for [job]". Never open the caption with "Comment...".
  4. Pick the length by the engine. View reels: 25-35s song-led loop (Kaye). Comment reels: 60-75s agent job skit with an asset every 5-8s (Higgsfield).
  5. Mix per week (at 14 a week): 5 cinematic or music loops starring you · 4 agent job skits · 2 one-prompt tutorials · 1 long story short (75-120s) · 1 offer with a deadline · 1 trend explainer.
  6. One themed keyword per reel, tied to the feature ("comment STORYBOARD"). Offer the setup or a link for product reels, and prompts for creative reels. Speak it in the last 2 seconds as well as writing it.
  7. Two windows: product and skit reels at 9am-3pm ET (6:30pm-12:30am IST), creative and cinematic reels late US night. Weekdays heavy, Sunday off.
  8. Test with two different edits, never a stale re-post. Same day, different hook, keep the winner.
  9. Every few weeks, one tentpole film with no product talk until the end card. It is where the 1M+ reels come from on both accounts.
